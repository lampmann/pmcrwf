Attacks (draft)

NameAbilityPrFxHit+To Hit Damage+modDmg+Roll
Ability sets the modifier for to-hit and (with +mod) damage; Finesse uses the higher of Str/Dex. Hit+/Dmg+ take flat numbers or dice (e.g. +1d6). Shift-click to hit for advantage, Ctrl for disadvantage, right-click for a menu. Fx folds in your feature effects (Sharpshooter, Rage, Divine Strike, …) — untick it on a weapon a bonus doesn't apply to; a button whose number includes one is underlined, hover it for the breakdown.

Offensive Routines (draft)

Program a turn once, then fire it with one click — e.g. 2× Halberd (Extra Attack) plus 1× Halberd (Polearm Master bonus action). Attack steps come from the Attacks module; save steps cover save-based effects like Toll the Dead, showing the DC and which save the target makes. Shift-click run for advantage on every attack, Ctrl for disadvantage.
