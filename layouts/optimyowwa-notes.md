# Optimyowwa draft

Target: 1408 x 880 browser viewport at 100% zoom. Grid: 16 pixels.
All module positions and dimensions use whole grid units, with one unit
(16 pixels) between adjacent visible modules. Tabbed modules share a box.

The preset picker labels this arrangement **Optimyowwa (Draft)**.

## Review points

The main gameplay panels fit together in the target viewport, beneath the
app controls. Long spell lists, feature descriptions, movement tables and
creature stat blocks scroll inside their modules. The layout gives each
of these a visible panel rather than fitting every row or description
on screen at once.

HP, Armor Class, Speed and Saving Throws start visible. Saving Throws
shares its box with Ability Scores and Skills, so switching those tabs
necessarily hides Saving Throws until its tab is selected again.

Spellcasting and Features remain separate for consulting resources and
modifiers while choosing spells. Attacks and Offensive Routines share a
box. Inventory and its Tracker share another box.

Combat is parked outside the main gameplay area rather than removed
from the application. Additional lower-priority tab groups and the
panel sizes are draft choices for review.
